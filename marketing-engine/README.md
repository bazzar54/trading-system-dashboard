# Done-For-You Marketing Engine

**The offer:** We write a full month of social media posts, product descriptions, and emails for your small product business — delivered as finished files in 48 hours.

**Who this is for:** Small e-commerce product business owners — perfume, supplements, crafts, niche physical goods — who know they need marketing content but don't have time or budget for an agency.

**Price:** £299/month recurring | £99 one-off trial month

**What the client gets:**
- 30 social media posts (ready to copy-paste)
- 5 SEO product descriptions
- 5-email welcome sequence (with subject lines)

**How it works:** Client fills in a one-page intake form. We run the pipeline. Content is self-checked against a quality checklist. Finished files delivered within 48 hours.
