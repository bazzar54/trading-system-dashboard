POST 23 — PROBLEM-SOLVING
If your fragrance fades by lunchtime, you're probably applying it wrong.

A few things that actually help:
- Apply to pulse points (wrists, neck, behind ears)
- Apply right after a shower, before getting dressed
- Don't rub — just let it dry
- Moisturise first — fragrance clings to hydrated skin

Try it. Most people find their scent lasts twice as long.
