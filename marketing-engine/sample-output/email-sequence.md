EMAIL 4 — EDUCATION
SUBJECT: How to actually choose a fragrance (honest advice)

Hello [FIRST NAME],

Most people choose fragrance wrong. Not because they have bad taste — but because no one ever told them how it actually works.

Here's what's worth knowing:

**Fragrance smells different on everyone.** Your skin pH, warmth, and body chemistry all affect how a scent develops. The same fragrance can smell completely different on two people. This is why you should always try before you commit to a full bottle — or ask someone who knows the range to steer you.

**Fragrance notes matter — but not the way you think.** The top notes are what you smell in the first five minutes. They're designed to attract you in a shop. But they're the least important part. What you're actually buying is the heart and base notes — what the fragrance smells like an hour in, and what it leaves on your skin at the end of the day. When we describe our fragrances, we focus on what they smell like after they've settled.

**More expensive doesn't mean better.** The fragrance industry has an extreme markup model. The actual formula cost of a high-end designer perfume is often under £10. The rest is packaging, distribution, and brand positioning. Independent fragrance brands use the same raw materials and manufacturers — the difference is where the margin goes.

**Less is more.** Two sprays to a pulse point (wrist, neck, chest) is enough. Don't rub — let it dry. Apply to moisturised skin for better longevity.
