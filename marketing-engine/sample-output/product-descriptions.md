Where mainstream brands charge a premium for dark florals of this calibre, Rose Noire is priced for regular wear, not just special occasions.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION — CEDAR & SMOKE

There are fragrances that smell like effort. Cedar & Smoke smells like ease.

Cedar forms the backbone — clean, dry, slightly earthy, immediately recognisable as a quality material.
