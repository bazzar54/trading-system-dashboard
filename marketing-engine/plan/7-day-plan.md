# 7-Day Plan — First Paid Job
## Done-For-You Marketing Engine

**Goal:** Land one paying client (minimum £99 trial) by end of Day 7.
**Time commitment:** Max 1 hour per day.

---

## Day 1 — Send the first message (30 mins)

**Do:**
- Set up a simple business email address (Gmail is fine — e.g. doneforyoumarketing@gmail.com or your own name)
- Send Message 1 (from outreach/first-contact-messages.md) to your dad
- Reply to any existing messages or contacts who might be a fit

**Don't do:** Build anything new. Don't redesign the landing page. Don't spend today on anything that isn't sending that first message.

**End of day target:** Message sent to dad.

---

## Day 2 — Follow the response (20 mins)

**If dad said yes:**
- Reply with the intake form (pipeline/intake-form.md) as a copy-paste or simple document
- Tell him it takes 15 minutes to fill in and you'll start the moment you have it back

**If dad hasn't replied:**
- Send a short follow-up: "Dad — sent you something yesterday, let me know what you think"
- Also post Message 3 in one UK small business Facebook group

**End of day target:** Intake form sent OR first Facebook post live.

---

## Day 3 — Build the sample (1 hour)

**When you have the intake form back:**
- Paste the completed intake form into the pipeline/generate-content.md prompt in Claude
- Run the pipeline — let it produce all 30 posts, 5 descriptions, 5 emails
- Save the output into sample-output/ (replace the placeholder files with real content)
- Run the quality checklist (pipeline/quality-checklist.md) — fix anything that fails

**If you're still waiting for the intake form:**
- Use the existing sample pack (Scent & Soul) as your proof of concept
- Post Message 3 in a second Facebook group

**End of day target:** A complete content pack ready to deliver, or proof of concept ready to show.

---

## Day 4 — Deliver and review (30 mins)

**Send the finished content pack to dad:**
- Email him the files (or share via Google Drive / WeTransfer)
- Ask for honest feedback: What's good? What feels off? What would you change?

**Do not wait for perfect feedback before moving on — keep the pipeline moving.**

**Post Message 3 in one more Facebook group if you haven't yet.**

**End of day target:** First delivery made. Feedback requested.

---

## Day 5 — Expand the outreach (45 mins)

**Ask dad to send Message 2 to his network** — forward it as-is, or adapt it. This is potentially your fastest route to customers who already trust you by association.

**Post Message 3 in two more Facebook groups** (search: UK small business, UK e-commerce sellers, Shopify UK, handmade sellers UK).

**Send Message 4 to 3 LinkedIn profiles** — find small UK product business owners. Look for people with physical products and thin social media presence.

**End of day target:** 5+ outreach messages sent. Dad's network message forwarded.

---

## Day 6 — Follow up (30 mins)

**Check every message you've sent:**
- Any Facebook group responses? Reply within the hour — fast response rate matters
- Any LinkedIn replies? Keep the conversation short and offer to send the sample pack
- Any response from dad's network contacts? Send them the sample pack immediately

**If you have a warm lead:** Offer the £99 trial. Keep it simple — "One month, £99, you'll have the content in 48 hours. Want to go ahead?"

**Don't negotiate on the trial price.** £99 is already significantly reduced.

**End of day target:** At least one warm conversation active.

---

## Day 7 — Convert (30 mins)

**Follow up every warm conversation from Day 6.**
- Short, direct message: "Did you get a chance to look at the sample? Happy to answer any questions — if you want to go ahead it's £99 and I can start the moment you send the intake form."

**Send Message 5** to 2–3 local independent shops near you. A quick visit works better than a message if you can manage it.

**End of day target:** One client who has paid or committed to pay £99 for a trial month.

---

## What a win looks like by end of Day 7

One paying trial client at £99. That's it. Not a big contract. Not a monthly retainer. Just one person who has paid you for one month of content — proving the model works.

If that person gets results, they pay £299/month. If they tell three friends, you have a pipeline.

---

## If Day 7 produces zero paying clients

Don't stop. Here's what to do in Week 2:

1. **Ask your dad for a quote** — even if his content isn't finished yet, ask him to say one honest sentence about the process. Use it as your first testimonial on the landing page.

2. **Offer one completely free month** to one business you genuinely want to help. Deliver exceptional work. Ask for a testimonial and permission to share the content as a case study. Then charge the next person.

3. **Send 10 more LinkedIn messages** — vary the opening line, keep the same honest tone.

4. **Post once per day in a different Facebook group** for 5 days. Vary the post slightly each time.

5. **Talk to people in person** — mention it at work, at the gym, anywhere. "I've built a marketing content service for small businesses — do you know anyone who might need it?" is enough.

The first paying client is always the hardest. Once you have one — even at £99 — the model is proven and the next one is easier.
