## YOUR OUTPUT — produce all of the following in order:

---

### PART 1: 30 Social Media Posts

Produce exactly 30 posts in the following mix:

**10 Product Spotlight posts** — each focused on one specific product. Lead with a customer benefit, not a feature. End with a call to action (link in bio / DM us / shop now). 100–150 words each.

**8 Lifestyle / Aspiration posts** — paint a picture of the life the customer wants. Connect the product to a feeling, a moment, an identity. No hard sell. End with a soft CTA or question to drive comments. 80–120 words each.

**6 Customer Problem-Solving posts** — open with a relatable problem the target customer has, then position the product as the solution. Be specific about the problem. 100–150 words each.

**4 Behind-the-Scenes posts** — give a glimpse into the business: sourcing, packaging, the founder's story, how a product is made or chosen. Builds trust and connection. 80–120 words each.

**2 Promotional / Offer posts** — a clear, time-limited offer or reason to buy now. No hype. State what the deal is, who it's for, and exactly how to claim it. 80–100 words each.

Format each post as:
```
POST [NUMBER] — [TYPE]
[POST COPY]
[SUGGESTED HASHTAGS: 5–8 relevant hashtags]
---
```

---

### PART 2: 5 SEO Product Descriptions

For each of the client's hero products, write a product description of 150–250 words.

Each description must:
- Open with the customer benefit (not the product name)
- Include 3–5 naturally placed keywords relevant to what someone would search
- Describe sensory details (for fragrance: notes, mood, occasion)
- Include one specific differentiator (what makes this product different)
- Close with a direct purchase prompt

Format each as:
```
PRODUCT DESCRIPTION — [PRODUCT NAME]
[DESCRIPTION]
KEYWORDS USED: [list them]
---
```

---

### PART 3: 5-Email Welcome Sequence

Write 5 emails for a welcome sequence — sent to someone who has just subscribed or made their first purchase. The sequence must tell a coherent brand story across all 5 emails.

**Email 1 — Warm Welcome + Brand Story**
Subject line: [write one]
- Welcome the new subscriber/customer warmly
- Tell the founder's story in 2–3 short paragraphs (why this business exists)
- Set expectations for what emails they'll receive
- No hard sell in this email

**Email 2 — Bestseller Spotlight**
Subject line: [write one]
- Introduce the single best-selling or most-loved product
- Explain why customers keep coming back to it
- Include a direct link prompt (write "[LINK]" as placeholder)
- Soft CTA to try it

**Email 3 — Social Proof / Reviews**
Subject line: [write one]
- Lead with a strong customer quote or review (write a realistic placeholder marked [REAL REVIEW TO INSERT])
- Build on it with 2–3 more testimonial formats (star rating, short quote)
- Reinforce the benefit the customers are experiencing
- CTA to shop or leave a review

**Email 4 — Education**
Subject line: [write one]
- Teach the reader something genuinely useful about the product category
- For fragrance: how to choose a scent, how to make it last longer, what fragrance notes mean
- Position the brand as knowledgeable and trustworthy
- No hard sell — end with an invitation to reply with questions

**Email 5 — First-Purchase Offer**
Subject line: [write one]
- Acknowledge they may still be considering
- Make a clear, specific offer (e.g. 10% off first order, free shipping, a bonus sample)
- Create mild urgency without fake countdown timers
- Single CTA button prompt: [SHOP NOW — LINK]

Format each email as:
```
EMAIL [NUMBER] — [TITLE]
SUBJECT: [subject line]
[EMAIL BODY]
---
```
